# 📍 Local-Lead-Scraper

**Find, analyze, and qualify local business leads (especially for home-service markets)** using AI and automation — all in one place.

This app isn’t just a basic scraper. It identifies **home-service businesses (contractors, landscapers, roofers, HVAC, plumbers, etc.)** that are strong prospects for:

- Website redesigns and builds  
- Google reviews automation & reputation management  
- Follow-up and outreach systems

It scrapes *local lead data*, *analyzes it*, and *scores it* so you know which businesses are worth pursuing.

---

## 🚀 What It Does

- 🔎 **Scrapes local business data** — names, categories, addresses, phone numbers, and more.  
- 📊 **Analyzes and qualifies leads** based on built-in criteria.  
- 🤖 **AI-powered scoring** to help prioritize the best prospects.  
- 🆓 Uses a **free Google API key** — enough free quota for most users unless heavily abused.

---

## 🧠 How It Works

This project uses **Google AI services** under the hood to enhance lead scraping with intelligent analysis and qualification, not just raw data collection. It’s designed to give you *better leads with less manual effort.*
